# KARIGARI Admin Dashboard: A 10/10 Strategy for SIH 26090

## The Problem Statement Reality Check
**SIH Problem Statement 26090** (MoSJE) specifically asks for two core deliverables:
1. **Multilingual Voice Cataloger**
2. **Dynamic Pricing Assistant**

Many teams fail because their "Admin Dashboards" are just generic charts showing fake sales data. To win, your Admin Dashboard must prove that your platform actually oversees and improves the two deliverables above. The Admin shouldn't just be looking at sales; they should be an **AI Quality Assurance and Pricing Guardian**.

Here is the exact flow you should give your teammate to build for the Admin Dashboard.

---

## The Winning Admin Dashboard Flow (What to Build)

### 1. The "Pricing Exploitation Flag" (Dynamic Pricing Oversight)
**Concept:** The AI suggests a price based on labor and materials. If an artisan manually overrides that price and accepts a much *lower* price, they might be getting exploited by a middleman or undervaluing their work.
**What the Admin Sees:** 
* A queue of "Pricing Flags".
* A table showing:
  * **Product:** Handwoven Odia Saree
  * **AI Suggested Fair Price:** ₹4,500
  * **Artisan's Accepted Price:** ₹2,000 🔴 *(Flagged: 55% below fair wage)*
* **Action:** The Admin (or NGO Facilitator) can click "Investigate" to call the artisan and ensure they aren't being exploited, or "Approve Override" if the artisan just wanted to run a discount.
**Why Judges Love This:** It proves your AI Pricing Assistant isn't just a blind calculator. It acts as a protective shield for marginalized workers, directly addressing the social impact requirement of MoSJE.

### 2. The "Voice Catalog QA Review" (Multilingual Cataloger Oversight)
**Concept:** AI translation can sometimes make mistakes. The admin needs a way to audit the Voice-to-Text pipeline.
**What the Admin Sees:**
* A "Catalog QA" tab for newly submitted products.
* A side-by-side view:
  * **Column 1 (Original Audio):** A play button to hear the artisan speaking in Odia/Hindi.
  * **Column 2 (Raw Transcript):** The Odia/Hindi text captured by Whisper.
  * **Column 3 (AI English Listing):** The final SEO-optimized English description generated by Gemini.
* **Action:** The Admin can quickly read the translation, ensure the AI didn't hallucinate (e.g., mistaking "cotton" for "silk"), and click "Publish".
**Why Judges Love This:** When judges ask, *"What if the AI hallucinates or translates poorly?"*, you can show them this screen. It proves you have built human-in-the-loop safeguards for the AI Voice Cataloger.

### 3. "Digital Inclusion" Impact Metrics
**Concept:** Instead of showing "Total Sales", show metrics that prove your solution works for illiterate users.
**What the Admin Sees:**
* **Chart 1:** "Catalogs Generated via Voice vs. Manual Typing" (e.g., 95% Voice, 5% Typing).
* **Chart 2:** "Average Time to List Product" (e.g., 2 minutes via Voice vs. 15 minutes manual).
* **Chart 3:** "Top Regional Languages Used" (e.g., 40% Odia, 35% Telugu, 25% Hindi).
**Why Judges Love This:** These metrics directly measure the success of the PS 26090 mandate. You are proving that the voice cataloger is actively breaking down the digital divide.

## Instructions for Your Teammate

Tell the teammate building the Admin Dashboard to implement a Next.js page with three tabs:
1. **Pricing Oversight** (Table comparing AI Price vs Final Price).
2. **Catalog QA** (Table showing Original Audio/Text next to the English Translation).
3. **Impact Analytics** (3 simple charts showing Voice vs Text usage).

**Tech Stack for Teammate:**
* Use `recharts` for the Impact Analytics charts.
* Use a simple HTML `<audio>` tag for the QA review if you want to play back the blobs (or just show the transcript text if audio blobs aren't saved to the DB).
* Fetch data from `prisma.craftItem`, comparing `fairWageFloor` (AI price) against `salePrice` (Artisan price).

If they build exactly this, your Admin Dashboard will perfectly bookend the Artisan App, creating a closed, flawless loop for the judges to evaluate.
